## Buyer profiles (who actually buys this)

- **The solo property manager:** Manages a portfolio of units for several owners, handling leasing, maintenance and owner statements personally.
- **The growing management company:** Adds doors and staff quickly and needs per-property and per-owner numbers that do not get lost in the growth.
- **The owner-relations focused manager:** Competes on trust with the owners who hired the company and wants every owner statement to be clean and easy to defend.
- **The maintenance-heavy portfolio manager:** Coordinates vendors and repairs across many units and wants to see which ones are actually worth keeping.

## The real pain points (use these to open / agitate)

- **Owner statements built by hand:** Pulling together rent, expenses and fees for each owner every month eats time and invites mistakes.
- **Management fees that blend together:** When fee income is not tracked separately from owner-collected rent, it is hard to see what the business itself actually earns.
- **Vacancy cost that goes unmeasured:** A unit sitting empty between tenants is a cost owners will ask about, and it is easy to underestimate.
- **Maintenance requests with no running cost:** Repairs get coordinated and paid for, but the total cost per unit rarely gets added up.
- **Lease renewals that slip past:** A lease that expires without a renewal conversation can turn into an unplanned vacancy.
- **Units that cost more than they earn:** Some managed properties take more maintenance and owner attention than their fee income justifies.

## Common mistakes this audience makes (good cold-open material)

- Building owner statements from memory instead of records
- Mixing management fee income with owner-collected rent
- Treating every managed unit as equally worth keeping
- Letting a lease expire before the renewal conversation happens
- Underestimating what an empty unit actually costs
- Not adding up maintenance cost by unit over time
- Waiting too long to chase unpaid rent or fees
- Leaving receipts and owner records scattered until tax time

## What it tracks (real KPIs, for the proof/features section)

- **Management fee income:** What the management company actually earns, separate from the rent collected on owners' behalf.
- **Vacancy cost:** What an empty unit costs in lost rent between tenants.
- **Unit profitability:** What each managed unit nets once its own maintenance and costs are counted.
- **Lease renewal rate:** How many expiring leases get renewed instead of turning into vacancies.
- **Owner distributions:** What is actually paid out to each property owner after fees and expenses.
- **Accounts receivable:** Rent and fees billed but not yet collected.

## Objections to pre-empt (and the honest answer)

- "My property management software already does this." — Property management software runs leasing, maintenance tickets and tenant communication. This workbook is the owner-facing financial view: statements, fee income, vacancy cost, per-unit profitability and tax set-aside. They can run side by side.
- "I am not an Excel person." — The dashboards and formulas are built; you log rent, expenses and fees. Each edition includes a Field Guide and a Quick Start. It needs desktop Excel.
- "I only manage a handful of units." — Essential covers the core: property setup, rent and expense entry, vacancy tracking, property P&L, a dashboard and tax set-aside.
- "Which edition do I need?" — Essential (12 sheets) covers property setup, rent and expense entry, vacancy tracking, property P&L, a dashboard, tax set-aside and an accountant export. Elite (31 sheets) adds tenant and lease tracking, maintenance, owner tracking and analytics, unit profitability, receivables, cash flow forecasting and benchmarks.
- "Will it get me more owner clients?" — No. It shows your numbers so you can run cleaner statements and decide which units are worth managing.
- "Does it send the owner statements for me?" — No. It is an analysis workbook, not statement delivery or accounting software.
- "Is it a subscription?" — No. It is a one-time Digistore24 purchase of a downloadable Excel product, not a recurring charge.
