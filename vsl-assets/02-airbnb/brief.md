## The real pain points (use these to open / agitate)

- **Revenue that hides the real profit:** Payouts look healthy until cleaning, platform fees, supplies and repairs are subtracted.
- **Properties that do not pull their weight:** With several listings, one weak property can quietly drag down the whole portfolio.
- **Occupancy without context:** A full calendar at a low nightly rate can earn less than fewer, better-priced nights.
- **Cleaning and maintenance costs:** Turnovers and repairs are frequent, small and easy to lose track of.
- **No clear return on the property:** Hosts often know the payout but not the return on what they invested.
- **Tax season paperwork:** Booking income, expenses and deductible costs end up scattered across platforms and receipts.

## What it tracks (real KPIs, for the proof/features section)

- **Occupancy rate:** The share of available nights that were booked.
- **ADR (average daily rate):** Average booking revenue per booked night.
- **RevPAR:** Revenue per available night — occupancy and nightly rate combined into one number.
- **Profit per property:** What each listing earns after cleaning, fees, supplies and repairs.
- **Return on investment:** Profit compared with what you put into the property.
- **Cleaning cost per turnover:** What each changeover costs, which adds up fast with short stays.

## Objections to pre-empt (and the honest answer)

- "Airbnb already shows my earnings." — The platform shows payouts. This workbook adds expenses, cleaning, maintenance, property profitability, ROI and tax set-aside. If payouts are all you need, you may not need it.
- "I am not an Excel person." — The dashboards and formulas are already built; you log bookings and expenses. Each edition includes a Field Guide and a Quick Start. It needs desktop Excel.
- "I use a property management tool." — Those tools run bookings and messaging. This is an owner's analysis workbook for profit, ROI and taxes. They can work side by side.
- "Which edition do I need?" — Essential (10 sheets) covers properties, booking revenue, expenses, property performance, tax set-aside and a dashboard. Elite (25 sheets) adds occupancy, ADR and RevPAR analytics, cleaning and maintenance, ROI, rankings and a profit leak detector.
- "Will it increase my bookings?" — No. It does not touch your listings. It shows your numbers so you can decide what to change.
- "Does it connect to Airbnb?" — No. It is an Excel workbook; you enter or paste your booking data.
- "Is it a subscription?" — No. It is a one-time Digistore24 purchase of a downloadable Excel product, not a recurring charge.
