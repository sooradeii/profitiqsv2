## Buyer profiles (who actually buys this)

- **The owner-operator:** Runs the mower and the route personally, quotes jobs from experience and does the books at night.
- **The multi-crew company:** Runs several crews and trucks across daily routes and has no side-by-side view of route or crew profit.
- **The program-based company:** Sells season-long fertilization, weed control, aeration and overseeding programs on top of mowing and needs each one tracked on its own.
- **The office or route manager:** Tracks routes, contracts, equipment and receivables for the owner.

## The real pain points (use these to open / agitate)

- **Routes that do not pay their way:** Drive time and stop count vary route to route, and some routes earn far less per hour than others.
- **Season-long programs tracked as one lump:** Fertilization, weed control, aeration, overseeding and irrigation each run on their own season and margin, and lumping them together hides which ones pay.
- **Mowing and program contracts lapsing at renewal:** Recurring mowing routes and season-long programs that reach their renewal point without contact can quietly end.
- **Crew productivity is invisible:** Owners know which crew feels fast, not which one covers the most route for the hours it works.
- **Equipment, vehicle and fuel costs creep:** Mowers, trucks, fuel and fertilizer all draw down cash in small amounts that add up by season end.
- **Tax-time paperwork:** Equipment, fuel, payroll and chemical receipts end up scattered by year end.

## Common mistakes this audience makes (good cold-open material)

- Treating every route as equally profitable
- Letting mowing and program contracts lapse at renewal
- Judging crews by how busy they look instead of route output
- Pricing routes without counting drive time between stops
- Running fertilization, aeration and overseeding without tracking each program on its own
- Letting equipment, vehicle and fuel costs creep unnoticed
- Leaving receipts in a drawer until tax season
- Not setting money aside for taxes

## What it tracks (real KPIs, for the proof/features section)

- **Revenue per route:** What each route brings in for the time and drive between stops.
- **Recurring revenue:** Revenue from mowing routes and season-long programs that repeats each cycle.
- **Crew productivity:** How many stops or how much billed work each crew completes per hour on the route.
- **Program renewal rate:** The share of fertilization, aeration and overseeding program customers who renew for the next season.
- **Equipment and fuel cost ratio:** Equipment, vehicle and fuel costs as a share of revenue.
- **Accounts receivable:** Money customers owe for completed visits and programs.

## Objections to pre-empt (and the honest answer)

- "My route app already tracks my stops." — Route apps run the stop list. This workbook is the owner's view of route profit, recurring revenue, crew performance, seasonal programs, equipment and fuel costs, receivables, cash flow and taxes. If your app already shows that, you may not need it.
- "I am not an Excel person." — The dashboards and formulas are built; you log service visits and expenses. Each edition includes a Field Guide and a Quick Start. It needs desktop Excel.
- "I only run one or two crews." — Essential covers the core: service visits, expenses, a dashboard, a health snapshot and tax set-aside.
- "Which edition do I need?" — Essential (9 sheets) covers service visit revenue, expenses, tax set-aside, a dashboard and a health snapshot. Elite (45 sheets) adds leads, customers, service contracts, recurring revenue, route management, route density, mowing, fertilization, weed control, aeration, overseeding and irrigation tracking, crew performance, equipment, vehicle and fuel tracking, labor, receivables, cash flow and forecasting.
- "Will it get me more customers?" — No. It shows your numbers so you can decide which routes, programs and services to focus on.
- "Does it schedule my crews or plan my routes?" — No. It is an analysis workbook, not routing or scheduling software.
- "Is it a subscription?" — No. It is a one-time Digistore24 purchase of a downloadable Excel product, not a recurring charge.
