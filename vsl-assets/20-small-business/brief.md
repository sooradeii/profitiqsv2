## Buyer profiles (who actually buys this)

- **The service or consulting firm:** Bills clients for time or projects and wants one clean view of revenue, expenses and margin without an industry-specific tool built around job costing or crews.
- **The business that does not fit a trade niche:** Runs a retail, wholesale or specialty business that is not a cleaning crew, a repair bay or a construction job, and has no use for job costing or crew tracking.
- **The owner running a few small revenue streams:** Has more than one way the business makes money and wants them rolled into one dashboard instead of tracked separately in different places.
- **The owner starting simple:** Wants to build the habit of tracking revenue, expenses and tax set-aside before deciding whether an industry-specific system is worth the extra sheets later.

## The real pain points (use these to open / agitate)

- **Revenue and expenses in separate places:** Bank app, invoicing tool and a shoebox of receipts rarely add up to one clear picture.
- **No single health check:** A busy month and a profitable month are not always the same month, and without a system it is hard to tell them apart.
- **Profit leaks hide in the details:** Small cost creep spread across several categories rarely shows up until it is already a pattern.
- **Customers tracked unevenly:** Some customers get remembered and some get forgotten, with nobody ranked by what they actually generate.
- **Unpaid invoices drift:** Money customers owe sits open longer than it should when nothing tracks how old it is.
- **Tax season surprises:** Deductions, estimated payments and a set-aside amount get worked out from memory instead of from the year's actual numbers.

## What it tracks (real KPIs, for the proof/features section)

- **Net margin:** What the business keeps after every expense, as a share of revenue.
- **Business health score:** A single score that rolls revenue, margin, cash and other signals into one number you can track month to month.
- **Profit leak:** A cost or margin problem the Profit Leak Detector flags because it looks out of line with the numbers around it.
- **Accounts receivable aging:** How long unpaid customer invoices have been open, grouped into buckets.
- **Revenue by customer:** How much of total revenue comes from each customer, so the important ones are visible.
- **Tax set-aside:** An estimate of what to hold back for taxes based on the year's numbers so far.

## Objections to pre-empt (and the honest answer)

- "My bookkeeping software already shows this." — Bookkeeping software is built for compliant records. This workbook adds a business health score, profit leak detection, customer ranking and tax set-aside planning in one place. If your software already answers those, you may not need it.
- "I am not an Excel person." — The dashboards and formulas are already built; you enter revenue and expenses. Each edition includes a Field Guide and a Quick Start. It does require desktop Excel.
- "Why not get the industry-specific version?" — If your business matches one of ProfitIQS's industry-specific systems — a trade, a clinic, an agency, a shop — that version will fit better because it already has the job costing, crew tracking or client pipeline your industry needs. This system is for businesses that do not fit one of those, or owners who want one simple system without the extra sheets.
- "Which edition do I need?" — Essential (9 sheets) covers revenue, expenses, deductions, a dashboard and a business health snapshot. Elite (27 sheets) adds a health score, profit leak detection, forecasting, benchmarking, customer analytics and a full tax center.
- "Will this grow my business?" — No tool can promise that. The workbook shows your numbers clearly; what changes is up to the decisions you make with them.
- "Is this just a generic template?" — It is built to run on real numbers for any small business rather than one industry's workflow. The sample data runs about $833K in revenue with a 16.9% net margin over a 12-month rolling window, so you can see how it behaves before you enter your own.
- "Is it a subscription?" — No. It is a one-time Digistore24 purchase of a downloadable Excel product, not a recurring charge.
