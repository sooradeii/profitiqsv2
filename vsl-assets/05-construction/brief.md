## The real pain points (use these to open / agitate)

- **Jobs that look profitable until they close:** Costs land after the estimate and the real margin shows up only when the project is finished.
- **Change orders that never get billed:** Extra work agreed on site can go unpriced or unbilled.
- **Work in progress blind spots:** Billing ahead of or behind the work done distorts how healthy the business looks.
- **Cash flow between draws:** Paying crews and suppliers before the next payment arrives strains cash.
- **No comparison across projects:** Without a portfolio view, it is hard to see which kinds of job actually pay.
- **Tax-time paperwork:** Job costs, receipts and subcontractor payments end up scattered by year end.

## What it tracks (real KPIs, for the proof/features section)

- **Job cost to date:** Everything spent on a project so far, against its budget.
- **Gross margin per project:** What a project keeps after its direct costs, as a share of the contract.
- **Work in progress (WIP):** Whether you have billed more or less than the work actually completed.
- **Change order value:** Extra work agreed after the original contract.
- **Backlog:** Contracted work not yet completed.
- **Accounts receivable:** Money owed on invoices and draws already billed.

## Objections to pre-empt (and the honest answer)

- "My estimating software already does this." — Estimating tools build the bid. This workbook tracks what actually happens: job costs, WIP, change orders, receivables, cash flow and tax set-aside. If your current tools already show that, you may not need it.
- "I am not an Excel person." — The dashboards and formulas are built; you log projects and costs. Each edition includes a Field Guide and a Quick Start. It needs desktop Excel.
- "Job costing takes too long." — Fair. The workbook needs costs entered against projects. Contractors who already collect receipts by job will find it straightforward.
- "Which edition do I need?" — Essential (8 sheets) covers project entry, expenses, tax set-aside, a dashboard and a health snapshot. Elite (25 sheets) adds the project portfolio, job cost center, WIP, change orders, project profitability, receivables, cash flow and forecasting.
- "Will it make my jobs more profitable?" — No tool can promise that. It shows job costs and margins clearly so you can bid and manage with real numbers.
- "Is it full construction accounting?" — No. It is an owner's analysis workbook, not accounting or project-management software.
- "Is it a subscription?" — No. It is a one-time Digistore24 purchase of a downloadable Excel product, not a recurring charge.
