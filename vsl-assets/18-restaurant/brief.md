## The real pain points (use these to open / agitate)

- **Prime cost blind spot:** Food cost and labor cost together decide whether a restaurant survives, and many owners only ever look at revenue.
- **Food waste quietly eating margin:** Spoilage, over-prep and portion drift happen every shift and rarely get logged anywhere.
- **Menu items that look popular but lose money:** A dish can sell well on the ticket count and still carry a food cost that leaves almost nothing after labor.
- **Labor scheduled against a guess:** Shifts get staffed against last week's memory instead of the sales pattern the day actually has.
- **Vendor price creep:** Invoice prices move up a little at a time and rarely get checked line by line against what was quoted.
- **Tax-time scramble:** Cash tips, sales tax collected, and deductions end up scattered across registers, statements and a drawer of receipts.

## What it tracks (real KPIs, for the proof/features section)

- **Prime cost:** Food cost plus labor cost as a percentage of sales, the single number that decides whether a restaurant is viable.
- **Food cost percentage:** What food costs as a share of food sales.
- **Labor cost percentage:** What labor costs as a share of sales.
- **Covers:** The count of guests served in a period, the base number behind every per-guest average.
- **Average check:** Revenue divided by covers, what each guest actually spends.
- **Food waste cost:** The dollar value of food spoiled, over-prepped or thrown out, tracked apart from the cost of food actually sold.

## Objections to pre-empt (and the honest answer)

- "My POS already shows me sales." — POS systems report sales and tickets well. This workbook is the owner's view across daily sales, prime cost, menu item cost, labor, food waste, vendors, cash flow and taxes in one place. If your POS reports already answer those, you may not need it.
- "I am not an Excel person." — The dashboards and formulas are built; you log daily sales, expenses and costs. Each edition includes a Field Guide and a Quick Start. It needs desktop Excel.
- "I do not have time to enter data every night." — That is a fair concern. The workbook needs daily sales and expenses entered or pasted in, and the Daily Manager Review sheet is built as a short end-of-shift habit, not a long one.
- "Which edition do I need?" — Essential (9 sheets) covers daily sales, expenses, tax set-aside, a dashboard and a health snapshot. Elite (28 sheets) adds menu cost, labor, reservations, food waste, vendor performance, daily manager review, cash flow, a KPI scorecard, forecasting, benchmarking and a dedicated prime cost center.
- "Will this lower my food cost?" — No tool can promise that. It shows prime cost, menu item cost and food waste clearly so you can decide what to change on the menu, in the kitchen or on the schedule.
- "Does it price my menu for me?" — No. The Menu Cost Calculator shows what a dish costs to make against what it sells for. Pricing the menu is still your call.
- "Is it a subscription?" — No. It is a one-time Digistore24 purchase of a downloadable Excel product, not a recurring charge.
